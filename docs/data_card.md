## Limits

- No demographics (gender, handedness, height, phone model) in this dump, so no fairness slices.
- Start-of-trial seconds may not match the labeled activity; trim is a later repair flag.
- Accel and gyro clocks are not assumed to share sample instants; alignment is a later repair step.
